# Voice-over

One voice at a time, about 140 words in total. Music ducks under every line (`voice` in `cut.json`).
Only the pitch is spoken on camera. The other lines go to `footage/vo/<name>.wav`, recorded dry, mono, 48 kHz.

| at | file | line |
|---|---|---|
| 0:05 | on camera | Thirty-five years ago I was twelve, drawing games I could never build. Today I type one sentence, and Diorama turns it into a painted world you can play. Let me show you. |
| 0:57 | how.wav | Muse Spark writes the world and its story. Muse Image paints every place. Spark looks at the painting, and Muse fixes only what's wrong. SAM 3.1 cuts out every object, so you can walk behind them. Then a bot plays it to the end. |
| 1:27 | studio.wav | Want a change? Just ask. The agent edits the world, rebuilds it, and checks it still plays. Undo is one click. |
| 2:03 | techniques.wav | From the masks come the floor, the scale and who stands in front. Depth gives shadows and parallax. MusicGen writes the score. |
| 2:30 | thanks.wav | Thanks for watching. It's open source. Go build the game you dreamed of. |

The hook, the world montage and the terminal have no voice.
